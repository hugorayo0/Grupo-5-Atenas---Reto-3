FAQ

Este apartado ofrece un FAQ para resolver las dudas más frecuentes sobre el proceso de fabricación de los vehículos, los tiempos de producción, el transporte, el control de calidad y el funcionamiento general de la fábrica.

How long does it take from the time you order the car until it is manufactured?

The waiting period is between two to three months.

How are cars transported once they have been manufactured?

Once manufactured, the cars are transported by lorry.

How long does it take for a car to reach the dealership after leaving the factory?

Once the car leaves the factory, it takes a maximum of 10 days.

How many cars are produced at the factory each day?

The factory produces approximately 500 cars per day.

What types of vehicles are manufactured at the plant?

The plant manufactures sedans, SUVs, and electric vehicles.

How many employees work at the factory?

Around 1,200 employees work at the factory.

What safety measures are followed during production?

All workers follow strict safety protocols and wear protective equipment.

Where do the car parts come from?

Most parts come from local suppliers, while some are imported.

How is quality control carried out?

Each car undergoes several inspections before leaving the factory.

How long does the assembly process take for one car?

The assembly process takes about 18 hours per vehicle.
